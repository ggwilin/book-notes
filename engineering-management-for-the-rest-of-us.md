## Part 1: Your Team.
### Caring for your team
- Your work is enabling everyone around you, it started about the value you brought to the team.
- Your team should be flow-driven.
- As an engineer you have done the work, so tasks are easy to understand.
- You have to make sure evwry one in your team understand why their work is important, and what their career path is going to be, its unique for everyone.
- You should understand power imbalance, people and consider strategies
- Caring is vital to doing this job well.

### The value of values
- Its the first step to understan what drives and motivates people, mutual understanding is a must.
- Individual values: fundamental beliefs that guide us, motivate us and drive our actions.
- The help us to determine whats important, what kind of persion we want to be.
- Examples: autonomy, compassion, collaboration, creativity, family, fun, growth, perfection, powe, vision, wealth, success, teamwork.
- Exercise: examine the list and pick 3, mine are: empathy, teamwork, versatility, why? just think.
- Values as context: They provide us context to understand whats happening and why.
- Conflict: misalignment of values, if we sahre values, this can build trust and vulnerability on a team, we are humans nonetheless.
- Exercise: Pick 5 values of a list, ask individually why, if group is shy share yours, this will help understand why they do some things.
- Do this with your peers as well.
- Having things in common, or understanding why not, its key.
